Task order for a harness launch, from gradle's own dry run.

The temporal premise the provider inventory rests on is that the resource is generated
BEFORE the harness JVM starts. That is the half gradle can show: processGametestResources
and any prepare task are ordered before runGameTest, which is the task that launches the JVM.

--- ./gradlew runGameTest --dry-run (stock) ---
  :compileJava SKIPPED
  :processResources SKIPPED
  :classes SKIPPED
  :compileGametestJava SKIPPED
  :deleteGameTestRunDir SKIPPED
  :acceptGameTestEula SKIPPED
  :generateLog4jConfig SKIPPED
  :generateDLIConfig SKIPPED
  :configureLaunch SKIPPED
  :processGametestResources SKIPPED
  :gametestClasses SKIPPED
  :runGameTest SKIPPED

--- ./gradlew runGameTest -PauditedTierHarness --dry-run ---
  :compileJava SKIPPED
  :processResources SKIPPED
  :classes SKIPPED
  :compileGametestJava SKIPPED
  :deleteGameTestRunDir SKIPPED
  :acceptGameTestEula SKIPPED
  :generateLog4jConfig SKIPPED
  :generateDLIConfig SKIPPED
  :configureLaunch SKIPPED
  :extractDiagonalBlocks SKIPPED
  :processGametestResources SKIPPED
  :gametestClasses SKIPPED
  :prepareAuditedTierGameTestHarness SKIPPED
  :runGameTest SKIPPED

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW: that no write occurred between the JVM reading the resource and the
post-run capture. There is no other writer to build/resources/gametest during a run -- the
roster's own delete happens at the top of the NEXT iteration -- but that is an inference from
the absence of a writer, not an observation of the read. Post-run observed content and
inferred run-time content are labelled separately in the packet for that reason.
